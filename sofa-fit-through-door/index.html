Will my sofa fit through the door?

Measure your sofa or couch at its maximum width, height and depth, then compare those dimensions with the doorway’s clear opening. Use the free calculator to test straight and rotated rectangular orientations before delivery day.

A sofa can fit even when its overall width is wider than the door

The sofa’s full length does not always need to cross the doorway side-to-side. Movers may lead with the depth or another dimension and rotate the piece. The important question is whether one of the sofa’s rectangular faces can clear the opening with enough room to maneuver.

A doorway calculation cannot prove that the sofa can make a tight turn immediately before or after the opening.

How to measure a sofa for a doorway

1. Measure the sofa width or length

Measure from the furthest fixed point on one side to the furthest fixed point on the other. Include arms or frame details that cannot be removed. Do not assume the retailer’s headline size is the exact delivery dimension; use the product specification or your own measurement.

2. Measure the sofa height

Measure from the floor to the highest fixed point. If the feet are removable, first record the sofa as delivered and then record a second height only if the manufacturer confirms that the feet can be removed safely.

3. Measure the sofa depth

Measure front to back at the deepest fixed points. For a sofa that may enter on its end, depth and height are often the key cross-section dimensions, but the calculator checks all three possible rectangular faces rather than assuming one carry method.

4. Measure the doorway’s clear opening

Open the door fully and measure the narrowest usable width plus the usable height. Frames, stops, handles and thresholds can take away space. The dedicated doorway measuring guide explains this in more detail.

5. Check the space around the doorway

A sofa may fit inside the opening but still need a large area to turn. Look at the hallway width, wall opposite the door, ceiling height, stair rail and any corner immediately before or after the doorway. If the result is tight, a professional mover or retailer should confirm the route.

Use a safety margin. Exact-equal measurements are not comfortable delivery measurements. Small measuring errors, upholstery, protective wrapping and hands all need space.

What does the sofa fit calculator test?

The main calculator takes your three sofa dimensions and forms three rectangular faces: width × height, depth × height, and width × depth. It then samples rotations from 0° to 90° for each face and compares the rotated bounding width and height with the doorway after the safety margin. If you also enter room dimensions, it checks whether the sofa’s width-by-depth footprint fits the room in normal or rotated orientation.

What if my couch only fits at an angle?

A rotated result means the calculator found a 2D rectangular orientation that clears the door opening. In real life, that move requires enough free space to reach and maintain the angle. A wall directly opposite the door, a tight landing or a stair rail can make the maneuver impossible even when the opening itself is large enough.

What should I measure for a sectional sofa?

If the sectional separates into individual modules for delivery, measure and test the largest rigid module separately. Do not enter the full assembled sectional size unless it is actually transported as one piece. Confirm the manufacturer’s delivery configuration before relying on any fit calculation.

What about sofa beds and recliners?

Measure the item in its delivery configuration, including fixed mechanisms and arms. Sofa beds and recliners can have rigid internal frames that do not compress like cushions. If removable backs or other parts are part of the manufacturer’s approved delivery method, test both configurations and keep the more conservative result in mind.

Sofa doorway checklist

  1. Sofa width or length at the widest fixed points.
  2. Sofa height at the highest fixed point.
  3. Sofa depth at the deepest fixed point.
  4. Door clear width at the narrowest usable point.
  5. Door clear height.
  6. Space needed to turn before and after the doorway.
  7. Any stairs, elevators, gates or internal doors on the route.

SOFA FAQ

Common sofa fit questions

Can a sofa fit through a 30-inch or 32-inch door?

There is no universal yes or no answer because sofa dimensions and the actual clear opening vary. Measure the specific sofa and the clear doorway, then test the dimensions. A sofa may fit with a different dimension leading through the opening.

Can I remove sofa legs to make it fit?

Only if the manufacturer or retailer confirms that the legs are designed to be removed safely for delivery. If so, measure the reduced configuration and run a separate check.

Does tilting always make a sofa fit?

No. Tilting can help at the doorway, but you also need enough room to create the angle and complete the move. Tight hallways, stairs and corners can still prevent delivery.
