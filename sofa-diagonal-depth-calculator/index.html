Calculate the sofa cross-section diagonal

The formula is diagonal = √(height² + depth²). Use the same unit for both measurements.

IMPORTANT

A diagonal measurement alone does not prove a sofa will fit

The calculation above is the diagonal of a simple height × depth rectangle. Real sofas may have arms, curved backs, protruding frames, removable feet or a shape that is not rectangular. A doorway move also needs room to create and maintain the angle.

What does sofa diagonal depth mean?

How to calculate the diagonal depth of a sofa

Measure the sofa height and front-to-back depth in the same units. Square each number, add the squares, then take the square root. For a 34-inch-high and 38-inch-deep sofa: √(34² + 38²) = √2600 ≈ 51.0 inches. This is the geometric corner-to-corner diagonal of the rectangular cross-section, not a guarantee that the sofa will pass through a doorway.

For this tool, diagonal depth means the straight-line diagonal across a rectangular cross-section formed by the sofa’s height and depth. Mathematically, it comes from the Pythagorean theorem. If a sofa is 34 inches high and 38 inches deep, the geometric diagonal is about 51 inches.

How to measure height and depth

Measure height from the lowest delivered point to the highest fixed point. Measure depth from the furthest fixed point at the front to the furthest fixed point at the back. If the manufacturer confirms that feet or another component are removed for delivery, calculate the delivered configuration separately.

How to tell if the sofa will fit through a doorway

Do not compare the diagonal alone with one door measurement. Measure the sofa’s width, height and depth, then measure the doorway’s clear usable width and height. The full sofa and furniture fit calculator checks all three rectangular faces at multiple rotated angles. Also check hallway corners, stairs, landings and turning space.
