DOORWAY MEASURING GUIDE

How to measure a doorway for furniture delivery

Measure the clear usable opening — the actual space the furniture must pass through. Check width, height, hardware, thresholds and the room needed to turn on both sides of the doorway.

THE KEY MEASUREMENT

Do not use the door slab size as your doorway clearance

Open the door fully and measure between the closest fixed points the furniture must pass. Then measure from the floor or threshold to the lowest fixed point at the top of the opening. If either measurement changes across the opening, use the narrowest usable value.

Step-by-step: measuring a doorway for a sofa or furniture

1. Open the door fully

Measure the route in the same practical condition you expect on delivery day. The door itself and its hardware can reduce the opening when it is not completely out of the path.

2. Measure the clear width

Measure horizontally across the narrowest usable gap between fixed obstructions. Look at frames, door stops, hinges, handles or trim that project into the opening. If the width changes from top to bottom, use the narrowest point relevant to the furniture’s path.

3. Measure the clear height

Measure vertically from the floor or highest threshold point to the lowest fixed obstruction at the top of the opening. If the item may be tilted upright, also check ceiling clearance immediately inside the doorway.

4. Check the threshold and floor level

A raised threshold changes the practical opening and can make a tight move harder. Note any step, lip or change in floor level that affects how the furniture must be lifted or angled.

5. Measure turning space before and after the door

The opening can be large enough while the approach is not. Measure the hallway or room width on both sides, especially if there is a wall directly opposite the door or a 90-degree turn.

For the calculator: enter the narrowest clear width and clear height. Add a safety margin rather than treating exact-equal dimensions as a comfortable fit.

What else on the route should I measure?

  • External gates and building entrances.
  • Narrow hallways and corridor turns.
  • Internal doors between the entrance and final room.
  • Stairs, rails, landings and low ceilings.
  • Elevator door opening and interior dimensions.
  • The destination room and its usable floor space.

How do doorway measurements work with sofa measurements?

Furniture has three main dimensions: width or length, height and depth. A sofa does not always cross the doorway in the same orientation in which it sits in the room. The free furniture fit calculator tests the three rectangular faces formed by those dimensions at multiple rotated angles and compares them with the clear opening.

Why a clear opening is better than a “standard door size”

Real usable openings vary because of frames, stops, hardware, thresholds, installation and the way the door opens. Measuring your actual route is more useful than relying on a typical or advertised door size. This is especially important when the furniture is close to the opening dimensions.

Doorway measuring checklist

  1. Door fully open.
  2. Narrowest clear width recorded.
  3. Usable clear height recorded.
  4. Threshold or step noted.
  5. Handles, frames and fixed obstructions checked.
  6. Turning space before the door measured.
  7. Turning space after the door measured.
